CNAs with outstanding RBPs

Descriptive only. RBP Policy v2.0.0 sets no numeric threshold, so there is no line here for a CNA to be over, and nothing on this page is a verdict.

Ordered by count, never by rate. Ranking by rate would put a five-person CNA above one publishing thousands of records a year, and no threshold exists that would justify it.

There is deliberately no rate column. Outstanding RBPs divided by records published is arithmetically the same quantity RBP Policy v1.0 attached its 5% and 50% sanction triggers to. Those thresholds were withdrawn with v2.0.0, but the v1.0 document is still mirrored by third parties and still ranks in search, so publishing that ratio against a named CNA would hand a reader a retired threshold to apply. Published records in the last 12 months are shown raw, as scale context only, so a CNA publishing thousands is not read the same way as one publishing fourteen.
