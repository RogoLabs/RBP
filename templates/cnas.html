Descriptive only. RBP Policy v2.0.0 sets no numeric threshold, so there is no line here for a CNA to be over, and nothing on this page is a verdict.

Ordered by count, never by rate. Ranking by rate would put a five-person CNA above one publishing thousands of records a year, and no threshold exists that would justify it.

The rate column is this site's own descriptive statistic for scale context, not a program limit. It is withheld entirely below {{ min_denominator }} published records in the trailing 12 months, because at that size a rate is noise. Where shown, the Wilson lower bound is given alongside so a small denominator cannot flatter or damn anyone.
