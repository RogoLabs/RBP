Which rule applies

RBP Policy v2.0.0 expects publication within {{ expectation_hours }} hours and points at two CNA Operational Rules sections that are not interchangeable.

{{ rule_must }} MUST
The owning CNA's own advisory feed carried it.

Every {{ rule_must }} reading here is a candidate, not an established breach. Ownership is always inferred for a reserved ID, because the reservation endpoint redacts it for exactly that population. Where the owner is unknown, the weaker rule is the only honest reading, so {{ rule_should }} is the default.

The Program used to publish this

A quarterly Reserved but Public table appeared on the CVE Metrics page in February 2021. It was commented out of the page on 7 February 2022, after about a year public. The markup is still in the site source, frozen, its last column Q3 2021.

The Metrics page today reports published records and reserved IDs, and nothing on the overlap between them. RBP Policy v2.0.0 names “Program metrics and audits” as a way RBPs are identified, so the public face of that channel has been switched off for four and a half years. That is the gap this site fills. Read the policy.
