The Program used to publish this

A quarterly Reserved but Public table appeared on the CVE Metrics page in February 2021. It was commented out of the page on 7 February 2022, after about a year public. The markup is still in the site source, frozen, its last column Q3 2021.

The record shows routine maintenance rather than concealment: the v1.0 policy PDF was withdrawn thirteen days earlier pending a replacement, and the table was commented out as item 2 of a three-item table restructuring. The replacement has now arrived as v2.0.0. The series has not. The Metrics page today reports published records and reserved IDs and nothing on the overlap between them, which is the gap this site fills until the Program's own series returns.
