The policy, and what changed

RBP Policy v2.0.0, approved by the CVE Board on 13 August 2026.

What it expects

The policy states the expectation plainly:

A CVE Record should be published within 72 hours of either (a) disclosure by the CNA or (b) the CNA becoming aware of a third-party disclosure, as applicable.

and aligns itself to two CNA Operational Rules sections:

  • {{ rule_must }}: CNAs MUST publish within 72 hours of publicly disclosing a CVE ID they assigned. Past that, the CNA's Root may direct a CNA-LR to publish.
  • {{ rule_should }}: CNAs SHOULD publish within 72 hours of becoming aware that a third party disclosed.

Rule 4.5.3.5 is also relevant: CNAs MUST reject unused or unpublished CVE IDs. A long-lived reservation is not a neutral state under the rules.

What enforcement looks like

Four levers, which the Program “may take” and which “may be applied individually or combined”: Warning, Reservation Caps, Intervention, Formal Review. Remediation deadlines are set case by case by a TL-Root or Root.

There is no condition that triggers anything by itself. The previous policy had an automatic arithmetic trigger tied to a CNA's share of unpublished IDs, which anyone with the data could compute. Version 2.0.0 contains no numeric threshold anywhere in its text.

Discretion is defensible. Discretion exercised entirely in private is not distinguishable from no enforcement at all, and there is no public RBP list, no public record that a CNA was notified, and no public enforcement log.

The field that would let anyone check

Ask the CVE Services API about a published ID and it tells you who assigned it:

GET /api/cve-id/CVE-2026-26100
{"state":"PUBLISHED","owning_cna":"Nozomi"}

Ask about a reserved one and it does not:

GET /api/cve-id/CVE-2026-2574
{"state":"RESERVED","owning_cna":"[REDACTED]"}

The field is populated and served. It is masked on exactly the population the policy governs, even though CNA Rule 4.5.1.7 expressly permits the Secretariat to publicly identify the CNA who reserved a CVE ID 24 hours after public disclosure.

The metric that used to exist

A quarterly Reserved but Public table went live on the CVE Metrics page in February 2021 and was commented out on 7 February 2022, after roughly a year in public. The markup is still in the site source, frozen, its final column Q3 2021.

RBP Policy v2.0.0 names “Program metrics and audits” as one of the ways RBPs get identified. The public face of that channel has been switched off for four and a half years. This site publishes what that table would show.
