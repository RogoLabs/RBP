Only the first of those is good news. A row is counted as published only where the CVE List confirms a published record, taken from the resolution ledger rather than from the difference between two lists.

Rejected is not resolved. Rule 4.5.3.5 requires CNAs to reject unused or unpublished IDs, so rejection is lawful and is the likely end state for the oldest rows here. For anyone consuming the CVE List it is worse than an open RBP: the ID stays cited in advisories and no record is ever coming.

No longer listed means we do not know. A row leaves this list for reasons that have nothing to do with a CNA: a transient API error, a feed that failed or was truncated, a change to the feed set, a raised buffer, or a revised advisory date. None of those are a publication and none are counted as one.
